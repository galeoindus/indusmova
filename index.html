Source-based answers · Hindi & English

Wikipedia, understood.

indusmova turns a question into a clear, source-linked answer. Ask in your own words and it reads live from Wikipedia and Wikidata — a direct answer, the key points, a topic-wise breakdown and the facts, each one traceable back to the article it came from. Article breakdowns quote published source sentences; chat can add an optional AI explanation that is grounded in the selected source passages and carries numbered citations you can open.

Ask, don't hunt

One question returns a direct answer with the points that support it, instead of a list of links to sift through.

Every claim sourced

Facts come from the Wikipedia infobox and Wikidata, and each answer keeps the citations attached so you can check it yourself.

Read in Hindi or English

Ask and read in either language, with the article, its sections and its references kept together.

Save what matters

Bookmark answers and keep a reading history, stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere.
